# Data licence

The files under `data/export/` and the JSON served under `/data/` and `/api/v1/`
on the site are our own derived data: claim verdicts, counts, ages, health
metrics, year-participation tables, alias tables and links. They are released
under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**.
Attribution: "Data from {SITE_NAME} ({SITE_URL}), CC BY 4.0".

What is deliberately NOT in the data:

* issue or comment body text;
* comment authors or any usernames beyond the public repo owner / org login;
* email addresses of any kind;
* GSoC contributor (student) names;
* descriptions copied from the community GSoC dataset.

Upstream sources and their licences are listed in `docs/SOURCES.md`. In
particular the GSoC seed list comes from `nishantwrp/gsoc-organizations`
(code GPL-3.0, data licence not stated, permission requested — see
`NEEDS_OWNER.md`). We store only organisation names, years, categories,
technologies and official URLs derived from it, and we link back on every
organisation page.

GitHub data is used under the GitHub Terms of Service and Acceptable Use
Policies. GitHub is the source of truth; our copy may be stale.
